How this works
  1. After each shift, pick Shift, Training or Event, fill in the fields and tap Save entry. Tick the box under the shift note to log your standing training on the same date in the same tap.
  2. Tap any entry in a list to edit it. Shifts that repeat a date, time and apparatus are flagged as duplicates.
  3. On the Month tab, Task Book and the other standing fields carry over from last month, so only the Task Book count usually needs changing. There is also a button to add your standing training to every shift that has none.
  4. At month end, go to Generate and tap Word or PDF. The memo is built on the phone and opens in the share sheet. The app compacts spacing on its own to hold one page, and says so when a month genuinely needs two.
  5. Export from Settings now and then. Entries live only on this phone.
